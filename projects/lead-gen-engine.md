# Lead-gen engine

**The problem.** Most lead generation dies in the gap between a raw list and a prioritized one. You can find thousands of names. Knowing which twenty to call today is the whole game, and it's the part people do by hand.

**What I built.** A six-component Python system that closes that gap on its own. It scrapes prospects from search, runs each through an AI qualification agent that scores fit and filters out the noise, qualifies partners on a separate track, and delivers a prioritized digest to the inbox every morning. Results export clean, and a weekly tracker watches performance over time.

**How it's wired.** Claude-powered scoring sits at the center. The scraper feeds it, the qualifier ranks against a defined profile, and the digest surfaces only what's worth acting on that day. Zero manual work after setup.

**The result.** A daily list of the right prospects in priority order, built while I sleep. The judgment that used to take a morning happens before I've opened my laptop.

**Note.** Built for a client. Everything here is described generically by design; no names, no numbers, no identifying detail.
