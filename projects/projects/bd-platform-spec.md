# Unified BD platform spec

**The problem.** The individual tools worked, but they were separate. A pipeline dashboard here, a scoring engine there, an outreach generator somewhere else. What was missing was the thing a revenue org actually runs on: one platform where research, scoring, outreach, and pipeline intelligence live together.

**What I built.** A full product specification for a five-module platform, written for handoff to build:

- **Pipeline dashboard**: the single view of where every relationship stands
- **Import and scoring engine**: takes a list, scores every name against the ideal profile
- **Prospect research agent**: builds the case for each target before a human touches it
- **Outreach generator**: drafts in a specific voice, not a generic one
- **Pipeline intelligence**: reads the state of the funnel and says what to do next

**Why it's here.** A spec is what a revenue leader produces before a team builds. This one defines the modules, the data flow between them, and the decisions the system makes on its own versus the ones it hands to a person. It's the difference between someone who scripts a task and someone who architects the function.

**Status.** Specified end to end, written for a builder to execute against.
